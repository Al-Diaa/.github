## About

Al Diaa Group has designed and manufactured office furniture in Hama, Syria since 1998: desks, meeting tables, chairs, cabinets, school seats and more. Every piece is available in custom sizes and colors, built from metal and melamine-faced wood, with a product warranty where needed and after-sales service.
